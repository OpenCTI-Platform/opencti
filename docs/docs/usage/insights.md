# Insights and summaries (AI-powered)

**AI Insights** generates summaries of an entity or a container on demand: how its activity evolves, what recent reports
say about it, what may happen next, and what analysts changed on it. This page explains where to find it, what each
summary is based on, and what it needs.

!!! tip "Enterprise edition"

    AI Insights is available under the "OpenCTI Enterprise Edition" license.

    [Please read the dedicated page to have all information](../administration/enterprise.md)

## Why use it?

Before working on a threat or a victim, you usually read its latest reports, look at the evolution of its indicators
and targets, and check what changed recently. AI Insights does this reading for you and gives you a short text to start
from, with the sources it used.

!!! warning "Check what matters"

    Summaries are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Each one says what it is based on: verify important
    information in the underlying knowledge before acting on it.

## Where to find it

Click **AI Insights** at the right of the tabs of an entity or a container. A panel opens with one tab per summary.

| Where | Entity types | Tabs shown |
|:------|:-------------|:-----------|
| **Overview** tab of an entity | Threat actor (group and individual), Intrusion set, Campaign, Malware, Sector, Country, Region, Incident | Activity, Containers digest, Forecast, Internal history |
| Every tab except **Knowledge** and **Content** of a container | Report, Grouping, Case (incident response, request for information, request for takedown) | Container summary |

All tabs start generating as soon as the panel opens. Each one shows its text as it is written.

## Summaries

The summaries are written in your language: the language of your profile, else the platform language, else the
language of your browser.

### Activity

With an AI provider configured on the platform, Activity describes how the activity of the entity evolves:

- **for a threat** (threat actor, intrusion set, campaign, malware): its indicators and its victims over the last
  24 months, and the sectors, countries and regions it targets;
- **for a victim** (sector, country, region…): the reports and cases about it and the attacks targeting it over the
  last 24 months, and the intrusion sets and malware behind them.

The summary ends with an evaluation of the trend: **Increasing**, **Stable**, **Decreasing**, or **Unknown** when there
is not enough data.

With XTM One, the selected agent is asked to retrieve time-series statistics about related indicators, relationships
and sightings and analyze their trends. The request does not prescribe a 24-month window.

### Forecast

With an AI provider configured on the platform, Forecast uses the same data as **Activity** to describe what may
happen next: likely evolutions of the threat, or of the threats targeting the victim.

With XTM One, the selected agent is asked to retrieve ranked distributions of related entities and time-series
statistics, then assess expected activity based on current trends. The request does not prescribe a 24-month window.

### Containers digest and Container summary

On an entity, **Containers digest** summarizes the most recent reports and incident response cases that contain it.
It starts with the key findings and cites, for each statement, the report it comes from (title, date and author).

On a container, **Container summary** summarizes that container only.

Each summary reads the description and content of the containers, the text of their attached files, and the
entities they contain. For **Containers digest** with an AI provider configured directly on the platform (see
[Prerequisites](#prerequisites)), two options let you narrow the digest:

| Option | Effect |
|:-------|:-------|
| **Relative time** | Only containers created in the last 24 hours, 7 days or month. **None** keeps them all. |
| **Limit of elements** | The number of containers to summarize, at most 10. |

### Internal history

Summarizes what happened to the entity inside the platform: creations, updates, merges and deletions made by
analysts and connectors, based on its 200 latest history entries.

## Copy, regenerate, change agent

- **Copy to clipboard** copies the summary, to paste it into a report or a note.
- Summaries are kept for a while, so that opening the panel again is fast. The date under each summary
  tells you when it was generated. Click **Retry** to generate it again from the latest knowledge.
- With XTM One, a selector at the top of the panel lets you choose the AI agent that writes the summaries. Changing it
  generates the current tab again.

## Prerequisites

AI Insights needs Enterprise Edition, and one of these two AI back ends. An administrator sets them up.

=== "XTM One"

    When the platform is connected to [XTM One](../deployment/configuration.md#xtm-suite) (`xtm:xtm_one_url` and
    `xtm:xtm_one_token` are set), AI Insights uses the AI agents of XTM One.

    An administrator must accept the Filigran AI Terms: in **Settings > Filigran Experience**, row
    **XTM One (Agentic AI)**, click **Validate the Filigran AI Terms**. The same row lets you turn the AI features off
    later. Until the terms are accepted, users see the button but cannot use it.

=== "AI provider configured on the platform"

    Without XTM One, AI Insights calls an AI provider configured in the platform (Mistral AI, OpenAI or Azure OpenAI).
    See [AI Service configuration](../deployment/configuration.md#ai-service): the token is required.

    In **Settings > Filigran Experience**, the **Generative AI (AI Insight, NLQ)** switch turns the feature on or off.

You also need access to the knowledge of the platform (**Access knowledge** capability) to use it.

| What you see | Meaning |
|:-------------|:--------|
| No **AI Insights** button | AI features are turned off, or the entity type has no AI Insights. |
| The button shows an **EE** tag | The platform has no Enterprise Edition license. Clicking the button explains how to get one. |
| *Enable AI powered platform* when you click | No AI provider token is configured. Contact your administrator. |
| *Ask Ariane isn't activated yet* | The Filigran AI Terms have not been accepted yet. Contact your administrator. |
| *No agent available for this action* | XTM One has no agent for this summary. An administrator should configure one in XTM One. |
| *Not enough data to summarize the containers attached to the entity* | No container matches. Check that you can access the container, or a report or incident response case containing the entity, then reopen the panel. |

## Data and privacy

The platform, or the XTM One agent acting for the user, reads the knowledge with the permissions of the user who
requests the summary.

- **With XTM One**, OpenCTI sends the request (the identifier of the entity or container) to XTM One, and the agent
  reads the data it needs from OpenCTI with your permissions.
- **With an AI provider configured on the platform**, OpenCTI sends the data the summary needs straight to that
  provider: names, descriptions and statistics, container content and file text, and, for **Internal history**, the
  history entries, which name the users and connectors who made the changes.
